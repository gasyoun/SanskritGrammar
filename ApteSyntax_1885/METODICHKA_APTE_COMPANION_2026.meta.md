([`METODICHKA_APTE_COMPANION_2026.md`](https://github.com/gasyoun/SanskritGrammar/blob/main/ApteSyntax_1885/METODICHKA_APTE_COMPANION_2026.md)).

## Purpose
Plan a **thin printed companion booklet** to Apte's *The Student's Guide to Sanskrit
Composition* (1885 / Лихушина 2021) — calibration commentary, corpus frequency bands,
cross-references and book-drawn exercises — by *consuming* the line's existing
claims/corpus-layer infrastructure rather than rebuilding it, and to bring the line to
the [five-artifact shape](https://github.com/gasyoun/SanskritGrammar/blob/main/docs/CONVENTION_METODICHKA_ARTIFACT_SHAPE_2026.md)
the Kochergina line established.
