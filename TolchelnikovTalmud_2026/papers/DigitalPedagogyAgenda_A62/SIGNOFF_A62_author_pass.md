## 2. Substance flags carried (not fixed)

1. **§6 names two "largest" gaps.** Paragraph 1: "Audio is the largest"; paragraph 2 (bold): "The measurement gap is itself a gap, and the largest one." A human should decide which comparative stands, or reword one as "largest asset gap" vs "largest methodological gap".
2. **§7 cites a §3.4 that does not exist.** "§3.4's graded readers" (Waring & Takaki paragraph) and "§3.4's difficulty scorer (a named gap)" (readability paragraph); §3 in this manuscript is the four-row RQ table with no subsections. The references date from the H1464 pass against an earlier section layout and need retargeting (probably §6 "reading-pack and difficulty datasets are planned" or §5).
3. **§7 parenthetical "(non-Indo-European-adjacent, classical, morphologically rich) language".** Sanskrit is Indo-European; the intended contrast is presumably with the modern European L2s Nation's framework was built on. The phrase as written will draw a referee note.
4. **Eleven vs twelve.** Abstract: "lets eleven aspects be tested before any user study runs"; §4.3: "leaves the other eleven unfalsifiable"; but §4.1: "twelve aspects would sit unfalsifiable" and the register is twelve capability metrics. If the eleven excludes the on-ramp arm as RQ4's own testbed, one clause saying so would settle it.
5. **No conclusion section.** The introduction's question (do the assets teach, and how would anyone know) is answered across §4 and §6 but never gathered; the paper ends on related work and a data inventory. Adding a heading is outside a voice pass.
6. **Thesis block duplicates the abstract** almost verbatim (scaffold residue from the readiness-2 pass). Whether it survives to submission is a structural call.
7. **Abstract headline "RQ1 is already answered"** sits next to the §3/§7 clarification that τ = 0.887 measures two frequency-counting channels of one archive, not expert-vs-corpus, and PM3 still reads "no corrected order published". The headline is the author's existing claim and was left at its strength; a human should confirm it survives that clarification.
8. **Byline added (call 1)** while the 5/5 row still lists "venue + byline/ORCID remain"; the ledger row is left as is so the human ticks it, not the pass.
