## Known limitations

- The topic count is a delivery envelope, not a claim of complete verbal morphology coverage.
- The local embedding sidecar has not yet been proven on the Systema deployment host.
- A 5–10-student pilot can reveal usability and workflow defects, not population-level efficacy.
- Exact standalone price, production subscription provider activation and course inclusion list
  remain human commercial decisions after the pilot.

## Intended use and known misuse

Use this plan to execute the four named Wave-1 lanes without reopening the interview. Do not use
it as authority to activate production billing, publish source texts, infer unattested forms are
impossible, or start Sandhi without Emeneau.

## Maintenance and sunset

The plan stays active through the W4 pilot readout. Each execution handoff updates its owned
backlog row and the plan's handoff table with PR/release evidence. After a separate expansion
decision, this plan becomes the historical Wave-1 decision record and the successor plan is
linked here.
