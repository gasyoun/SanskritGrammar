## Purpose

The plan-of-record for the printed companion booklet (методичка) to Bühler's
«Руководство» (1923) — the second textbook companion after Kochergina (H807), fulfilling
the MO021-09 premise («методички ко всем санскритским грамматикам на русском»). The spec
fixes scope, inherited decisions, rights posture and open questions; the v1 manuscript
carries the actual Russian commentary.

## Audience

MG (author of record, pre-print visa); future sessions executing follow-on slices
(exercise appendix, отсылки-раздел, print assembly, v2); learners only indirectly
(through the eventual print/site output).

## Limitations

- v1 commentary covers only the 17 flagged claims + 24 badges; the 383 TRUE claims are
  uncommented by design (thin-curated ruling B).
- The government section inherits the 3.9 % dependency-arc ceiling of DCS — CONFIRMED
  is a floor, UNATTESTED-INSUFFICIENT is "unknown", never "absent".
- Whether each FALSE is a 1923 print corruption or a 2008 digitization defect is
  unresolved per row (B-1).
- The `methodichka` field fills mirror the manuscript notes in compressed form; the
  manuscript, not the field, is the print source.
