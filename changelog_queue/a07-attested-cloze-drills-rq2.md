### Added
- **A07 (Sonnet 5 `claude-sonnet-5`): attested-sentence cloze drills ship the third RQ2 auto-drill shape — sandhi-split and paradigm-fill already existed, cloze did not (25-09-2026).** [`docs/ROADMAP_DIGITAL_SANSKRIT_PEDAGOGY_2026_2028.md`](https://github.com/gasyoun/SanskritGrammar/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP_DIGITAL_SANSKRIT_PEDAGOGY_2026_2028.md)'s "Wave 2 — auto-drill generation with verified answer keys (RQ2)" row named three drill shapes (sandhi-split, cloze, paradigm-fill); the first two had already shipped (`scripts/build_emeneo_sandhi_drills.py`; the W2-add-a attested-cell declension trainer, H1296) but cloze had not. New [`sangram/data/attested_cloze_drills/build_attested_cloze_drills.py`](https://github.com/gasyoun/SanskritGrammar/blob/main/sangram/data/attested_cloze_drills/build_attested_cloze_drills.py) blanks one word out of an attested DCS sentence (4-12 tokens, first eligible NOUN case+number or finite VERB tense+mood+person+number target by word order) — the answer key needs no generation step to verify against because it IS the corpus attestation that produced the question; distractors are other attested forms of the same lemma in a different cell, never invented. Self-contained: depends only on the pinned VisualDCS DCS snapshot (no kosha, no vidyut), ~25 s to regenerate. 3,000 items / 1,007 distinct lemmas, 100% MCQ mode at the shipped `--min-lemma-freq 10` cutoff. Two correctness disciplines carried through from existing house findings: `feat_voice` emits `NonPass`/`Pass`, never `Act` (DCS cannot see the Parasmaipada/Ātmanepada split, VisualDCS `gen_paradigm_attested.py` H1299 precedent), and every VERB row's raw `feat_formation` is carried unmodified so an untagged Perfect default at `Past.Ind` is never silently presented as independently confirmed (the H3966 mood-guard finding). A same-surface-form distractor bug (neuter Nom.Sing=Acc.Sing syncretism, e.g. `karma`, made one MCQ item unanswerable) was caught by the test suite before landing and fixed by excluding the target's own form, not just its own cell, from the distractor pool. Ships a widget ([`AttestedClozeDrill.jsx`](https://github.com/gasyoun/SanskritGrammar/blob/main/src/components/talmud/AttestedClozeDrill.jsx)) and trainer page ([`sangram/articles/attested-cloze-drills/`](https://github.com/gasyoun/SanskritGrammar/blob/main/sangram/articles/attested-cloze-drills/index.mdx)), same "tool not article" status as the declension trainer (no `article.manifest.json`, no `toc_ref`). Tests: [`tests/test_attested_cloze_drills.py`](https://github.com/gasyoun/SanskritGrammar/blob/main/tests/test_attested_cloze_drills.py) (10 tests: pure-logic cell/target-picking fixtures + spot-checks that every sampled item's blank reconstructs the source sentence exactly and no distractor duplicates the answer).
