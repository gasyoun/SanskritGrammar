New [`sangram/data/unified_drills/build_unified_drill_bank.py`](https://github.com/gasyoun/SanskritGrammar/blob/main/sangram/data/unified_drills/build_unified_drill_bank.py) reads all three committed TSVs and re-derives a `verified` flag for every row from its own already-computed signal — corpus-attested agreement for declension, `rungs_json` step/depth consistency for samasa, unique-first-choice MCQ parity for sandhi — with **no fabrication or upgrade**: a declension row whose `agreement` is `mismatch`/`no_generation` is emitted as `verified=false`, not dropped or silently promoted.
